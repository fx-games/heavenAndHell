Actions the player needs to do:

 - Help - F1 / ?
 - Move & Attack - Numpad
   - Auto-explore & Auto-attack
 - Identify Stuff - (l)ook
 - Run - Shift + Direction
 - Rest - numpad 5 / .
 - Use stuff in the dungeon - int(e)ract + Direction
   - Open doors / Close doors
   - Disarm traps
   - Ascend / Descend Stairs
   - Pick up
   - Kick doors

 - Pick up by walking over it

 - Equip/unequip items - E
 - Throw items
 - Shoot missiles

 - Use Abilities/Items - (a)pply -> Choose ability/item
   - Eat food
   - Drink potions
   - Read scrolls
   - Use wands
	   - With a target


 - Targeting
   - Auto-target
   - Cycle through targets
   - Remember last target
   - Quick-Shot nearest target
   - Directional quick-shot
