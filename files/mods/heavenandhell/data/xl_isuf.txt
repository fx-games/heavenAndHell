## xl_isuf.txt: the magic item suffixes, Diablo's PL_Suffix table, one tab-separated row each, ended by a row whose power is
## IPL_INVALID. Lines starting with ## are comments; the first other line names the columns.
## Columns:
##   PLName      the suffix as it reads in the item's name ("... of the Leech")
##   PLPower     the power it grants: an IPL_ name (IPL_TOHIT, IPL_DAMP, IPL_STR, IPL_LIGHT, ...), or one of the
##               engine's HOOK_ powers (HOOK_MANA_ON_KILL, HOOK_FIREBALL_ON_SHOT, HOOK_RETURN_DAMAGE, HOOK_TRIPLE_DAMAGE)
##               for an on-hit effect instead of a stat
##   PLParam1 / PLParam2   the power's value range, rolled between them
##   PLMinLvl    the lowest item level it rolls on
##   PLIType     the item kinds it may appear on, "PLT_WEAP|PLT_BOW|PLT_STAFF|PLT_ARMO|PLT_SHLD|PLT_MISC"
##   PLGOE       its alignment: 0 neutral, 1 evil (a curse), 16 good; one item never carries an evil prefix
##               and a good suffix together
##   PLDouble    1: it counts twice in the roll (twice as likely)
##   PLOk        1: it may also roll where only good powers are allowed (premium stock, quest rewards)
##   PLMinVal / PLMaxVal   the item's price at PLParam1 and at PLParam2 (interpolated between)
