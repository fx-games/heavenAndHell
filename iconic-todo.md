# Iconic touches (later)

Things that make it feel like Diablo, beyond the rules. None of these change gameplay.

## 1. Voice lines

- "The sanctity of this place has been fouled." (D1)
- "Stay a while, and listen." — Deckard Cain (D1)

## 2. Music

- D1: Tristram
- D2: Lut Gholein

Music is wired up: every `.ogg` in `files/assets/music/` shows up in the music player (Esc → Audio → Music Player),
and entering an area plays `town.ogg`, `cathedral.ogg`, `catacombs.ogg`, `caves.ogg` or `hell.ogg` when it exists.
Voice lines go in `files/assets/voice/` and play at the voice volume.
