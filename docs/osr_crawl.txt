OSR Approach
low-level
low-fantasy
low-power
Dungeon Crawl with a hint of simulationism
Player usually underpowered
Straight up combat is usually a bad idea
Offer lots of non-combat, non-magic solutions

Give meaning to adventuring gear:
 - Ropes over chasms and for climbing up/down
 - Torches for light and fireplaces, throwing for exploration and combat


Combat should be simple and transparent and doesn't have to be balanced.
Instead, there should be options to deal with opponents without fighting.

In any given situation, the player should have at least 3 options:
 - Fight
 - Flee
 - Be tricky

Stuff that should be possible
 - Pushing a boulder to block a door
 - Using a rope to get over a chasm
 - Triggering a trap with a thrown item
 - Trap a door
 - Sacrificing a shield to block a heavy blow
 - Ignite flammable floor with a torch
 - Impale a monster with a closing portcullis
 - Let the floor/ceiling collapse on a monster
 - Manipulate a monster into fighting another monster
   - Lure with food/noise/yourself/gold
   - Charm with magic
   - Parley with social skills
   - Bribe with gold/items
 - Push monster down a chasm/into a trap
 - Running away
 - Teleporting away
 - Sneaking past
 - Causing a distraction

ENVIRONMENTAL DAMAGE!
