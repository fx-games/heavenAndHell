Game
====

0. From Rogue: The 2D random grid based dungeon, move to attack, turn based
1. From Angband, Diablo 1 & 2: Plot, Setting, Quests & Loot
2. From ToME: Juiciness, No Mana & Health potions, but instead the inscriptions system
3. From Brogue or DCSS: Weapons have specific attack patterns (axes are different from swords)
4. From that other Roguelike: Maneuvers, using the environment to your advantage


Weapons
=======

1. Axes
   - 1H: 1 tile in front, 1 tile to the left and right
   - 2H: Can attack all 9 tiles around the player

2. Polearms / Spears / Staves
   - 1H: Range 2, When moving into an adjacent tile, polearms get a free attack
   - 2H: Range 2, When moving into range (1-2 tiles away), polearms get a free attack

3. Daggers / Gauntlets / Claws
   - 1H: Will always apply weapon procs 100% of the time, ignore 1/2 armor (Buckler max shield)
   - 2H: Will always apply weapon procs 100% of the time (twice), ignore armor

4. Maces / Hammers
   - 1H: 50% chance to reduce enemy armor by 1
   - 2H: 100% chance to reduce enemy armor by 1

5. Swords
   - 1H: Synergy with shields, increased chance to block & hit
   - 2H: Can attack all 9 tiles around the player

6. Shields
   - Give chance to block
     -> Block Chance : Clamp(0, 100, Dex(defender) + 2 * (Level(defender) - Level(attacker)) + ClassBonus(defender))
   - Can be used to charge, to inflict knockback, stun, knockdown, etc

Direction
=========

1. Remove the tedium
  - Fast entry into the game, fast travel, etc.
  - Time from start to first kill < 1 minute
  - No food, no instadeath, no hidden doors
  - Simple controls
  - Fast progression, no grinding
  - Prevent just using the strongest skill over and over again

2. Must feel good to play
  - Juiciness
  - Sound
  - Responsive Controls
  - Constant dopamine hits
  - Enable combos and synergies for the player to break the damage ceiling

3. Play your build
  - Build should not depend on random drops
  - All items usable by all classes, given the stat requirements
  - Items that don't fit your build can be stripped for their inscriptions

4. Classes / Races / Skills / Items?
  - Choose your class -> starting gear / attributes
  - Allow to choose a subset of class specific skills, that will be ensured during the run
    - Although their relative strength is not guaranteed, their sum of power is
  - Items can be used by all classes, given the stat requirements
  - Items will drop with skills from every class

Angband
=======

Good:
 - Town
 - Loot
 - Descend/Ascend at own pace (also enables level scumming)
 - Clear & simple goal
 - Shopping
 - Level feelings (but that also means people are just going to skip bad rolls)
 - Uniques
 - The use of light sources & food (but they are always needed, so reduce the inventory space)
   - Give them dedicated slots, that don't subtract from inventory space

Bad:
 - UI (especially Inventory/Storage management)
 - No active class Skills, only magic/no-magic & staves/wands/rods
   - Some class skills would be nice
 - No variation in gameplay even when leveling up or descending
   - The skills should get mechanically more powerful, not just the numbers
   - Different levels should offer different environmental challenges & puzzles & mechanics
 - Too long
   - Reduce the amount of levels
 - Environment is too static
 - Levels too empty and not varied enough
   - Smaller and more densely packed levels as a default
 - Resistance stacking is too important
   - Remove damage types completely and go with mechanical effects instead
 - Too complex for its own good
 - Enemies have unfair attacks (reduce stats, massive damage, damage equipment, burn scrolls, blind, stuns, paralyze, etc.)
   - Enable situational counters that don't depend on having the right "anti-item" in your inventory
   - Reduce the impact of these effects (only temporary, only on certain "brittle" items, etc.)
 - Many items become useless fast
   - Provide additional uses for beginner items
 - Vendors don't always offer neccessary items (potions, basic sling, etc.)
   - Basics should always be available
 - Having to grind for the final gear needed for the final boss
   - The final boss should be beatable with the gear you find on the way
   - But there should be the option to grind to a certain extent, to make the fight easier. this should be interesting though
 - Not being able to sell items just feels wrong
   - Allow selling items?
 - Boring Enemies
