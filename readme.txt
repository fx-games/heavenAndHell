Heaven & Hell
by Felix Ruzzoli

Heaven & Hell is a 2D top-down, turn-based, grid-based, single-player dungeon crawler heavily inspired by Angband and the original Diablo.

It is using procedural generation to create the dungeons.

This won't win any awards in terms of innovation anytime soon, I know.

I am re-using code I have written for some of my previous projects (https://github.com/memmaker/TerminalAssassin).

My goal was to have an Angband style dungeon crawler, with a modern UI and QoL features,
that can pleasantly be played in much shorter burst of time.

Features:
 - Line of sight
 - Pathfinding
 - Procedural generation
 - Class & Skill system
 - Inventory & Equipment
 - Loot System
 - Quests & Unique Monsters

Press "F1" during play for help.

Some mechanical intricacies:
 - No to-hit chance. By default, all attacks connect. Exceptions are made for special abilities (eg. flying/dodging)
 - No resistances/damage types. Damage is mitigated by armor.
 - No ammo pickups. Ranged weapons are assumed to always have ammo.
